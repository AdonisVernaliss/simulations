# Visual asset provenance

The simulation keeps observational imagery separate from procedural materials. The files below are redistributed at web-ready resolutions without altering their scientific content.

| Local asset | Source | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `public/textures/planets/earth-blue-marble.jpg` | [Blue Marble: Next Generation](https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/collection/1484/planet-textures) | NASA Earth Observatory |
| `public/textures/planets/mars.jpg` | [Mars image texture](https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/mars/) | NASA/JPL-Caltech; Viking imagery processed at USGS |
| `public/textures/planets/venus.jpg` | [Venus image texture](https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/venus/) | NASA/JPL-Caltech; Magellan radar imagery |
| `public/textures/planets/jupiter.jpg` | [Jupiter image texture](https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/jupiter/) | JPL-Caltech; Voyager imagery |
| `public/textures/sky/tess-all-sky-{low,balanced,high}.jpg` | [TESS 2022 all-sky cylindrical projection](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/14265/) | NASA/MIT/TESS and Ethan Kruse (University of Maryland College Park) |
| `public/textures/black-hole/{deflection,inverse-radius}.dat` | [Real-time High-Quality Rendering of Non-Rotating Black Holes](https://github.com/ebruneton/black_hole_shader) | Copyright (c) 2020 Eric Bruneton; BSD 3-Clause |

The generic rocky-body, Saturn, and ice-giant appearances are procedural renderer materials. They are visual models rather than observational surface maps. Full license text and redistribution conditions are retained in the repository's `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
